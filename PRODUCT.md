## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript (user's request). No framework, no build step, no backend. Four pages mirroring the original site architecture: `index.html`, `productos.html`, `nosotros.html`, `contacto.html`.

## Users

- Small and medium metalworking shops (talleres), contractors and industrial buyers in Barrancabermeja and the Magdalena Medio region who need industrial gases, welding consumables, oxy-fuel cutting equipment, fire extinguishers, safety gear and industrial hardware, often urgently and by phone.
- Companies that need extinguisher recharging or oxy-fuel equipment repair.
- Oxiferba employees, who use the site as a jump point to webmail (mail.oxiferba.com) and the FERCE cylinder system (ferce.oxiferba.com).

## Product Purpose

Oxígeno y Ferretería Barrancabermeja (OXIFERBA) sells oxygen and other industrial gases, industrial hardware, welding supplies, safety equipment and abrasives; sells and recharges fire extinguishers; and repairs, maintains and supplies parts for oxy-fuel equipment. The site exists to show the full catalog and services, build trust through history and clients, and get the visitor to call, message or visit the store. Success: a visitor finds what they need and contacts the store.

## Positioning

A family business founded in February 1996 by José Dolores Hernández Malavera, serving the regional market of small and medium workshops and contractors that the multinationals in the sector do not cover. Claims stated on the original site: "Somos los primeros en soldaduras especiales en la región", "Lleve su extintor recargado por cambio inmediato", "Somos la mejor alternativa para este servicio en la región". Motto: "Servicio con sencillez para un mutuo beneficio".

## Product Principles

1. Contact is never more than one tap away; phones and WhatsApp are first-class.
2. The catalog is the proof: show every line and item, scannable at a glance.
3. Honest and local: real photos of the store and team, real clients, no invented claims.
4. Fast on a phone over mobile data in Barrancabermeja's sun.
